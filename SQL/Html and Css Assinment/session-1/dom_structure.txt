Session 1: Task 4 - DOM Structure of myprofile.html

Below is the DOM tree structure representing the parent-child relationships of elements in myprofile.html (at least 3 levels):

html (Level 1)
 ├── head
 │    ├── charset (meta)
 │    ├── viewport (meta)
 │    ├── title
 │    └── style
 └── body (Level 2)
      └── div.profile-card (Level 3)
           ├── img.profile-img (Level 4)
           ├── h1#profile-name (Level 4)
           └── p (Level 4)

Relationships:
- html is the root parent element.
- body is a direct child of html.
- div.profile-card is a direct child of body and a grandchild of html.
- img, h1, and p are siblings to each other, children of div.profile-card, and descendants of body/html.
